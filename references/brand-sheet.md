# Brand sheet

Starting point pulled from the current generator settings
(`generate_background.py` → `STYLES`). Claude updates this after studying
the reels you drop in `references/`.

## Fonts
- **Headlines / hooks:** Fraunces (soft serif), semi-bold — `fonts/Fraunces-Variable.ttf`
- **Cards / numbers:** Inter SemiBold — `fonts/Inter-Variable.ttf`
- **One-off handwritten beat:** Caveat (sticky notes only)

## Captions
- Top third of the frame only (presenter owns the middle and lower two-thirds)
- Centred, no box or outline, subtle shadow
- Two looks per video at most: Editorial Hook + Analysis Card

## Pacing
- Hook on screen 0–4s
- Overlays pop on/off instantly (no fade creep)
- 6–8 overlays per video; build-up lists one word at a time, timed to speech
- Closing teaser in the last ~6s ("Comment X…" / "Next: …")
